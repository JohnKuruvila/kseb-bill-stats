KSEB Tariff Slabs 2026 (LT-1A) explained

A plain-language guide to Kerala's domestic KSEB tariff: the bi-monthly slab rates, the telescopic versus non-telescopic rule, every line item on your bill, and how to work out your real cost per unit.

Last reviewed: October 2026. Verify against the latest KERC tariff order.

KSEB domestic (LT-1A) slab rates

Most Kerala homes are on the LT-1A domestic tariff, billed every two months. For consumption up to 500 units in a bi-monthly cycle the billing is telescopic — each slab is charged at its own rate:

Telescopic vs non-telescopic: the 500-unit threshold

Above 500 units per bi-monthly cycle, the tariff becomes non-telescopic: the entire consumption is billed at a single flat rate (roughly ₹6.60 to ₹9.50 per unit) instead of progressive slabs. This is why a small increase in usage can produce a large jump in the bill.

Why is the KSEB bill bi-monthly?

KSEB reads most domestic meters and issues bills every two months. That is why the slab figures look "double" a monthly tariff: limits like 80, 160 and 500 units all apply per bi-monthly bill. When you compare your usage to a national average, divide by two for a monthly figure.

What each line on your KSEB bill means

Line itemWhat it is
Energy Charge (EC)Slab-based charge on the units imported / net consumed.
Fixed Charge (FC)Bi-monthly charge based on connection phase and sanctioned load.
Electricity Duty (ED)10% statutory levy on the energy charge.
Fuel Surcharge (FS)Per-unit adjustment notified periodically by KERC.
Meter Rent (MR)Bi-monthly meter rental, plus GST.
Other adjustmentsArrears, advance/ACD, or small statutory adjustments that can appear as their own line.

Your real cost per unit (₹/kWh)

The rate printed on a bill divides total charges by grid-imported units only. For a solar home that overstates the cost, because self-consumed solar never appears as an import. Your true cost per unit is:

total bill spend ÷ (grid import + self-consumed solar) kWh

The solar estimator and the full app compute this for you.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my KSEB bill bi-monthly?

KSEB bills most domestic consumers every two months, so each bill covers about 60 days and the slab limits are doubled (e.g. telescopic up to 500 units).

What is the difference between telescopic and non-telescopic billing?

Up to 500 bi-monthly units, slabs are progressive (telescopic). Above 500 units, all units are billed at one flat rate, so the bill jumps.

What components make up a KSEB bill?

Energy charge, fixed charge, 10% electricity duty, fuel surcharge, and meter rent with GST — plus any arrears or deposits.

How do I find my 13-digit consumer number?

It is printed at the top of your KSEB bill and every e-bill, and uniquely identifies your section and account.

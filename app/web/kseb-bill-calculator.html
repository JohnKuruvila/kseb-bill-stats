KSEB LT-1A domestic slab rates

Kerala domestic consumers are billed bi-monthly. For consumption up to 500 units in a cycle, billing is telescopic: each slab is charged at its own rate.

Worked examples

  • 160 units → energy ₹616, fixed ₹100, total ≈ ₹808.
  • 240 units → energy ₹1,040, fixed ₹180, total ≈ ₹1,362.
  • 500 units → energy ₹2,974, fixed ₹260, total ≈ ₹3,595.
  • 600 units (non-telescopic) → energy ₹3,960, fixed ₹280, total ≈ ₹4,710.

Notice how crossing 500 units removes the slab benefit and lifts the effective rate sharply — the single biggest reason two similar homes can get very different bills.

Frequently asked questions

How is the KSEB domestic bill calculated using telescopic slabs?

Units are billed progressively: the first 80 at ₹3.50, the next 80 at ₹4.20, and so on up to 500 units. If the cycle crosses 500 units, the whole bill switches to a single flat rate, so the total rises much faster than the units consumed.

What charges are added on top of the energy charge?

A bi-monthly fixed charge (depending on single- vs three-phase supply), 10% statutory electricity duty, a KERC-notified fuel surcharge per unit, and meter rent with GST.

Is this the official KSEB bill calculator?

No. It is an independent, free estimator and is not affiliated with KSEB. Always treat the bill served by your electrical section as authoritative.

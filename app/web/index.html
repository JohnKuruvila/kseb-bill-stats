How KSEB Solar Net-Metering Works

  • Bidirectional Meter: Measures energy imported from KSEB and excess solar energy exported back into the 230V/415V distribution grid.
  • Net Settlement: If grid imports exceed exports, you only pay energy charges for the net imported units at the LT-1A slab rate.
  • Unit Banking: If exports exceed imports, the surplus units are "banked" in your KSEB ledger and rolled over to offset subsequent bi-monthly cycles.
  • Annual September Settlement: At the end of every settlement financial cycle (September 30), remaining banked units are settled by KSEB at the KERC-approved Average Power Purchase Cost (APPC) rate.

KSEB Electricity Tariffs & Billing FAQs

Clear answers on Kerala electricity bills, slabs, solar net-metering & privacy

How is the KSEB domestic electricity bill calculated using telescopic slabs? കെ.എസ്.ഇ.ബി ബിൽ

In Kerala, domestic consumers under the LT-1A tariff are billed on a bi-monthly (2-month) cycle. For total consumption up to 500 units bi-monthly (250 units monthly), billing is telescopic. This means units are broken down and billed progressively across slabs:

  • First 80 units: ₹3.50 / unit
  • Units 81 to 160: ₹4.20 / unit
  • Units 161 to 200: ₹4.80 / unit
  • Units 201 to 280: ₹5.80 / unit
  • Units 281 to 300: ₹6.60 / unit
  • Units 301 to 400: ₹7.30 / unit
  • Units 401 to 500: ₹8.40 / unit

If bi-monthly consumption exceeds 500 units, the tariff becomes non-telescopic, meaning all units are billed at a single flat slab rate (e.g., ₹6.60 to ₹9.50/unit), causing a noticeable jump in the bill amount.

How does rooftop solar net-metering and banked unit settlement work? സോളാർ നെറ്റ് മീറ്ററിംഗ്

Under Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC) net-metering regulations, a bi-directional energy meter records both grid import and solar export units:

  • Net Consumption: When Import > Export, you pay energy charges only on the difference (Net Units) according to standard LT-1A slabs.
  • Banked Units: When Export > Import, the surplus units are recorded as banked energy and carried over to offset subsequent bi-monthly bills.
  • September Settlement: Banked energy is cleared annually at the end of the settlement year (September 30). Unadjusted banked units are compensated by KSEB at the notified Average Power Purchase Cost (APPC).
Where can I find my 13-digit KSEB consumer number? 13 അക്ക നമ്പർ

Your 13-digit consumer number is printed at the top of your printed KSEB electricity bill receipt and on official e-bills downloaded from the KSEB Web Self-Service portal. It uniquely identifies your electrical section and consumer account across Kerala.

What components make up the total KSEB electricity bill? ബിൽ ഘടകങ്ങൾ

A standard KSEB electricity bill consists of:

  • Energy Charge: Billed on imported or net consumed units according to the tariff slab schedule.
  • Fixed Charge: Bi-monthly charge based on your connection phase (single-phase vs three-phase) and sanctioned connected load.
  • Electricity Duty: 10% statutory levy on total energy charges under the Kerala Electricity Duty Act.
  • Fuel Surcharge: Periodic adjustment notified by KERC per unit consumed.
  • Meter Rent & GST: Bi-monthly rental fee for the electricity meter plus 18% GST.
How does this platform calculate the true cost per unit (₹/kWh)? യൂണിറ്റ് നിരക്ക്

Conventional bills only show cost divided by grid-imported units. For solar homes, this skews reality because self-consumed solar energy powers your appliances without ever touching the bill. This platform computes your True Unit Cost by dividing your total bill spend by your whole-home energy demand (Grid Imports + Self-Consumed Solar), giving you the actual cost per unit of running your household.

Is my KSEB data, consumer number, and password secure? സുരക്ഷ & പ്രൈവസി

Yes. This application was built with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • Sync requests connect directly and securely via HTTPS with the official KSEB Web Self-Service portal.
  • Any stored credentials are encrypted at rest using Fernet AES-128-CBC cryptographic keys.
  • The codebase is fully open-source for public verification.
  • You can download a complete JSON export of your data or permanently erase your account and all parsed bills with a single click.
